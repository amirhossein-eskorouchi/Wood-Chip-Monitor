# Code of Conduct

## Commitment

Wood-Chip Monitor is committed to a welcoming, respectful, and professional
environment for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience,
discipline, or career stage.

## Expected behavior

Participants should:

- communicate respectfully and constructively;
- welcome technical questions and differing viewpoints;
- give and receive feedback professionally;
- credit scientific and engineering contributions accurately;
- respect privacy, confidentiality, and intellectual-property boundaries;
- avoid harassment, intimidation, and discrimination; and
- prioritize research integrity and reproducibility.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable conduct includes harassment, discriminatory language, threats,
publication of private information, false attribution, deliberate inclusion
of credentials or malware, disruption, and retaliation against good-faith
reporting.

## Scope and reporting

This policy applies to issues, pull requests, reviews, project discussions,
meetings, and other project spaces.

Do not report sensitive conduct matters publicly. Contact the repository owner
through the GitHub profile and request a private communication channel.

Maintainers may remove inappropriate content or restrict participation when
this policy is violated.
